Island Night - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Island Night is an online multiplayer survival game. This page explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Email address - used only to sign you in (we email you a one-time code) and to tie your progress to your account.
  • Display name you type in the game, shown to other players in the same game room.
  • Game progress - diamonds, unlocked classes, statistics and the saved game you choose to save.
  • Technical data - your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse (rate limiting) and is not stored with your profile.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of your data, and no collection of your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Who processes data for us

The game server runs on Amazon Web Services. One-time sign-in codes are sent with Amazon Simple Email Service. On the website (not the app) you may instead sign in with Google or Apple, which share your email address with us when you choose that option.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time inside the game: on the main menu choose Delete account. You can also request deletion at the address below.
